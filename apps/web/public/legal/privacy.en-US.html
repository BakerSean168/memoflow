Privacy Policy

Version 2026.07.18 · Effective 2026-07-18 · Updated 2026-07-18

This Privacy Policy explains how MemoFlow collects, uses, and protects information related to you.

1. Data we process

  • Account data: email, verification state, sessions, and security events.
  • Product data: goals, tasks, notes, reminders, and other content you create.
  • Device and diagnostics: device type, coarse location if available, error logs, and performance metrics.
  • Optional integrations: GitHub identity or repository authorization metadata after you explicitly grant access.

2. Purposes

We process data to provide and improve the Service, secure accounts, enable sync and search, respond to support requests, and run AI features you enable.

3. Local vs cloud

Desktop guest mode and unbound local Vaults do not upload business content by default. Content reaches our servers or your authorized repository only after you enable sync or cloud features.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share data only with infrastructure, email, or model providers as needed to run the Service, or when required by law.

5. Retention and deletion

We retain product data while your account is active. After account closure we delete or anonymize data according to product flows, except security logs retained where required by law.

6. Your rights

You may access, correct, export in-scope data, or request account closure by contacting legal@memoflow.app.

7. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or a higher age required by local law).

8. Changes

When this policy changes we update the date above. Continued use after the update constitutes acknowledgment of the revised policy.
